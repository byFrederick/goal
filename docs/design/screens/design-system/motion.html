The ten rules

  1. 1Frequency decides. Hundreds-a-day actions get no animation; hover is colour only.
  2. 2Fast. 250ms or less; panels, sheets and toasts up to 400ms; exits faster.
  3. 3Ease out on enter, never ease-in. ease for colour, linear for constant motion.
  4. 4Transform and opacity only. Blur up to 3px; height through grid rows.
  5. 5Nothing appears from nothing. Start at scale .96 to .98 and opacity 0.
  6. 6Origin-aware. Menus grow from their trigger, dialogs from the centre.
  7. 7Interruptible. Reversible things are transitions on state, not keyframes.
  8. 8Name every value with a token. No raw ms, cubic-bezier or transition: all.
  9. 9Reduced motion. Drop transforms and blur, keep the state change.
  10. 10One ambient motion. The running dot pulses; nothing else moves on its own.
