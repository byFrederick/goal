Design system: foundations

Version 3, Plain. Colour, type, shape, space and elevation for every Goal screen.

FoundationsComponentsMotion

Principles

  1. Quiet by default, loud only for meaning.Colour only where it says something, mostly as a dot, a bar or a number.
  2. Contrast before colour.The primary action is near-black; selection is a grey fill; weight and size before hue.
  3. Density is a feature.13 to 14px text, 32px controls, tight rows and hairline rules.
  4. Show it, don't write it.A number with its target, a bar or a timeline before a sentence.
  5. People act where they read.Approve, Deny and Reply sit on the row that asks; one primary action per screen.
  6. Things move because someone acted.Short transitions for every change; only the running dot moves on its own.

What we never do

  • Gradients, glows, glassmorphism, tinted shadows, noise or grain
  • Purple or indigo as a brand colour; saturated fills behind large areas
  • A coloured left-border accent on cards or callouts
  • Emoji, sparkle icons or a magic glyph for agent features
  • Icon tiles as decoration in lists, timelines or empty states
  • More than four KPI tiles, or a KPI without a target or comparison
  • All-caps labels, eyebrow text, strings joined with middle dots
  • Radius above 10px except avatars, dots and dialogs
  • A dark hero panel inside a light page
  • Copy that explains the UI; filler empty states
  • Stretched or display typefaces in the app UI
