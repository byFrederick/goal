---

The factory and its flow come first. Read them in this order, or jump to the one you need.

- [Factory](/docs/concepts/factory/): the production line, keyed by its id, and the definition that describes it.
- [Work items and work types](/docs/concepts/work-items/): the units of work that move along the line, their states and their kinds.
- [Stations and the flow](/docs/concepts/stations-and-flow/): the steps of the line and the rules that move work along it.
- [Lanes](/docs/concepts/lanes/): how one station sends different kinds of work to different agents.
- [Gates and human touches](/docs/concepts/gates-and-human-touches/): how a review decides whether work moves on, and what people have to decide.
- [Rework](/docs/concepts/rework/): how rejected work goes back to an earlier station.
- [Assemblies and parts](/docs/concepts/assemblies-and-parts/): how one item splits into parts that ship together.
- [Integration branches](/docs/concepts/integration-branches/): how the parts of an assembly can land on the default branch together.
- [Expedite and priority](/docs/concepts/expedite-and-priority/): which waiting item an agent slot pulls next.
- [WIP and budget](/docs/concepts/wip-and-budget/): the caps on work in progress and on spending.
- [Andon](/docs/concepts/andon/): how a person stops the whole line.
- [Agents](/docs/concepts/agents/): a model with its prompt, skills and environment, working at a station.
- [Skills and rules](/docs/concepts/skills-and-rules/): the instructions agents load and the always-on guidance a factory gives them, standing rules included.
- [Access and secrets](/docs/concepts/access-and-secrets/): the organization's secrets, and which tools each factory reaches with them.
- [Knowledge](/docs/concepts/knowledge/): the folders of Markdown files agents read, and the factory's memory.
- [Jonah](/docs/concepts/jonah/): the daily review of the line that proposes changes a person approves.
- [Questions](/docs/concepts/questions/): how an agent asks a person and gets an answer.
- [Organization policy](/docs/concepts/organization-policy/): the model allowlist and denied commands that limit every factory.
