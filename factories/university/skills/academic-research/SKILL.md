# Academic research

- Source ladder: peer-reviewed journal articles, academic books, government and NGO reports, then reputable press.
- Evaluate each source: author credentials, venue, date, citations, method.
- Cite in APA 7 unless the syllabus says otherwise; keep DOI links.
- Quote sparingly and always with page numbers; summarize in your own words otherwise.
- Check the course's AI-use policy in the syllabus and respect it.
